## 📬 About me — Contact

My work sits at the intersection of **LLMs, Computer Vision, and Reinforcement Learning** —  
I enjoy designing agents that can reason, plan, see, and act with autonomy.  
Whether it's training models, crafting datasets, building pipelines, or deploying systems,  
I care about engineering that is clean, scalable, and grounded in real research.

I’ve worked across many parts of the AI stack:  
from transformer models and RAG pipelines to ROS2 robotics systems,  
from CV model training to hybrid RL + search algorithms.  
I’m constantly exploring how different branches of AI can be combined  
to create something more powerful than their parts.
